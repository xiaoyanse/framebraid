# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## English

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarized here.

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit permission, other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Report incidents to the maintainer via [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/xiaoyanse/framebraid/security/advisories/new) or by opening a security-scoped issue. All reports are reviewed and investigated confidentially.

## 中文

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant 行为准则 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)（上段为其要点摘译），适用于所有参与者和场所，包括仓库、Issue、讨论与线下代表项目的事件。

我们承诺让每个人免于骚扰地参与社区，不因年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向而区别对待。

不可接受的行为示例：性暗示语言或图像、恶意挑衅、侮辱或贬损言论、公开或私下骚扰、未经明确许可公开他人隐私信息，以及其他在专业场合被合理认为不当的行为。

维护者可删除、编辑或拒绝违反本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 及其他贡献，并会在适当时说明处置原因。

不当行为请通过 [GitHub 私密报告](https://github.com/xiaoyanse/framebraid/security/advisories/new)向维护者举报；所有举报都会被保密审阅与调查。
